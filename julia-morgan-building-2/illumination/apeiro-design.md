## Len Aurbach

* https://www.linkedin.com/in/s-leonard-auerbach-fastc-iald-14819541/

Founder and Emeritus

S. Leonard Auerbach FASTC, IALD, MIES

As Founder – Emeritus, Len celebrates over 50 years of professional experience and international expertise in theatre consulting, AV and lighting design. His approach to consulting combines his background in architecture with scenic and theatrical lighting design experience gained in New York and at leading regional theatres. In 1968, he left his position as resident lighting designer at the Guthrie Theatre in Minneapolis and joined the consulting firm of Bolt, Beranek, and Newman. In 1972, he formed the consulting firm of Piacentini/Auerbach that ultimately became Auerbach Pollock Friedlander – Auerbach Glasow, now Apeiro Design. Len has designed hundreds of venues worldwide working with high profile architects and clients, often developing the next technological standard for the industry. He provides a spirit of collaboration and ingenuity that has always been the foundation of the firm’s consulting practice. Len continues to be available to the firm to support special projects.


## Patricial Glasow

* pglasow@apeirodesign.com

Her strength lies in the blending of design and technical expertise particularly for specialized installations. She was formally trained in theatrical lighting design at UCLA and worked for Walt Disney Enterprises early in her career. These experiences deepened her passion for the profession and influenced her work for her arts, commercial and educational clients. Patty’s recent projects include Salesforce Tower Executive Floors, Haskins Biotech Campus, renovation of the historic Curran Theatre, Arizona State University Media and Immersive eXperience Center and the US Capitol Building Visitor Center Exhibition Hall Redesign. Her lighting has been recognized with awards from numerous industry organizations. She is a Fellow of the International Association of Lighting Designers and was recently awarded the Edison Report’s Lifetime Achievement Award for contributions to the lighting industry.
