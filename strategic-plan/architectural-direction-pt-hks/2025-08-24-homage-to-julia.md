# Homage to Julia

We can agree that the Julia Morgan building is a wonderful building. An interesting aspect to this is the homage given to Julia by her successors.

## The Stone Cottage

I cringe every time I look at the "Stone Cottage" at the end of the Bay Street parking lot. The stonework is totally the effort of an amateur.

Nonetheless, there are aspects that respect Julia's work.

* The windows are casement windows
* The roof is imitation slate, made out of yucky cement, but still slate-like

## Perry Building

The Perry Building, the four story stark Brutalist monolith, was designed by Warren Perry (1884-1980), Dean of the the UC Berkeley School of Architecture.

Perry pays homage to Julia in multiple ways.

* The building is oriented East-West
* The structure is reinforced concrete
* The windows are casement windows
* The external color is red brick
* The west elevation has four bricks insets that mimic the brick insets that resemble roses on the Julia Morgan building
* The the south elevation of the "Corridor" includes terracotta column capitals that contain rosebud elements.

## Health Center

The Health Center was designed by Gardner Dailey (1895-1967), a modernist architect who was one of two people who introduced modern architecture to Northern California. Dailey pays homage to Julia in multiple ways.

* Two of the roofs are slate on pitched roofs
* The building is oriented East-West
* The windows are casement windows
* The western end slightly mimics the sun room of the adjacent sun rooms of the Julia Morgan building
* Two of the wings are red brick
* The color of the stucco is red brick
* 2025-08-25: OMG, new observation: The gable and door reveals facing the courtyard are lined with terracotta tiles.
